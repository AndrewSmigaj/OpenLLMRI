12 scene settings per label per probe. Scenes are the units of within-label diversity AND of
scene-held-out cross-validation. No single scene exceeds ~15% of a label's pool. This file is
design-side (NOT shown to blind generation batches — they get one scene + the contrast spec).

# v2 expansion (8 September 2026) — families 13–24 per label

Same discipline as families 1–12: 25 distinct sentences per family per class,
no paraphrases, word "tank" banned in all tank context sentences, fiction
sub-arm rules unchanged. Design constraints for the new settings, adopted at
the v2 protocol review: each setting is distinct at the spec level from all
v1 settings (no near-synonyms of an existing setting); registers deliberately
overlap ACROSS classes (maintenance, retail, media, auction, industrial,
insurance registers appear on both sides of each contrast) so the axis cannot
shortcut on register; at least two new real-world families foreground real
acts of writing; no personal name may recur across families (audit-enforced).
